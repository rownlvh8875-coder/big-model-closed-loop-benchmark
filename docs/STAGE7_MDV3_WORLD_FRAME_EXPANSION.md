and existing model-body FRD to integrator-body FLU/world-CCW action bridge.

producer, reader, adapter, model spec, core module and workflow remains

Execution accounting distinguishes **committed parent steps** from total
executed `session.run` calls. The frozen parent commits ModelTiming only after
output validation and state commit. A failed policy/session/output-validation
attempt can therefore execute ORT without adding a trace/audit, even when all
earlier rows are well formed. Four committed traces plus a fifth successful
capture can mean four or five executed calls: the exact total is unknown.
The raw field `known_committed_parent_steps` preserves the established lower
bound; `total_executed_ort_calls` is JSON null when a failed uncommitted call
may have run. A zero committed count also does not prove zero executed calls.

The unchanged loop and validated capture/bridge order establish at most one
policy/session call per successful capture return, while committed traces
establish a lower bound. Equal bounds prove an exact total. This covers full
episodes, fully audited post-inference truth failures, setup before policy,
capture errors before policy and close after a completed episode when their
phase receipts support it. Ambiguous or malformed evidence yields an unknown
total with a concrete error. A possible first uncommitted official attempt
can likewise have unknown `official_model_execution`, rather than falsely
claiming that no inference executed.

Group/cohort summaries report the planned budget, attempted and unattempted
processes, `known_committed_parent_steps_lower_bound`, completed frame rows and
`unknown_case_count`. Any unknown attempt makes the aggregate executed-call
total null; committed lower bounds never become a fake exact1472 total.
Missing cases cannot be assigned zero execution. Accounting is not partial
gate acceptance. Malformed/incomplete/invalid cohorts always preserve a
diagnostic failure summary and exit nonzero. Complete repeat mismatches can
be successful data collection with an explicitly false exact gate.
Stale case/final summaries, wrong nonce/source/runtime/clock/renderer data or
reused UUIDs fail. A stale final snapshot is retained without recursive growth.
